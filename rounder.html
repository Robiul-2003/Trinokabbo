Our Founders’ Stories

At ecoeen, our journey is shaped by two visions united by a shared mission: to create a sustainable, socially responsible future through bamboo and jute products. Meet the minds behind ecoeen:

Shohel Hossen Preserving Heritage, Empowering Artisans Founder, ecoeen Growing up in the heart of Bangladesh, Shohel witnessed the delicate balance between rich cultural traditions and the rising tide of environmental challenges. Streets filled with plastic waste sparked a question that stayed with him: “How can we preserve our culture while protecting the planet?” With a background in Textile engineering, Shohel could have followed a conventional career path. Instead, he chose to revive age-old bamboo craftsmanship and turn it into a modern, eco-friendly movement. His vision was clear: create products that reduce plastic use while empowering local artisans with fair wages, skill development, and stable livelihoods. Under Shohel’s guidance, ecoeen has become a platform where heritage meets sustainability, where bamboo and jute products carry not just quality and design, but also hope and opportunity for communities across Bangladesh.
